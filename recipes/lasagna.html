Lasagna

Description

This is a classic lasagna recipe that is sure to impress your family and friends. It features layers of pasta, meat sauce, ricotta cheese, and mozzarella cheese.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1 pound Italian sausage
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 cans (15 ounces each) tomato sauce
  • 2 cans (6 ounces each) tomato paste
  • 2 cans (6 ounces each) tomato sauce
  • 1 can (15 ounces) ricotta cheese
  • 3 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 12 lasagna noodles
  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. In a large skillet, cook ground beef, sausage, garlic, and onion over medium heat until browned.
  3. Stir in tomato sauce, tomato paste, and tomato sauce. Simmer for 30 minutes.
